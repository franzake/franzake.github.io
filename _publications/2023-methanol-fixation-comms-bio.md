## Abstract

This study provides a systematic comparison of fixation methods for droplet-based single-cell RNA sequencing of neural cells. We demonstrate that methanol fixation preserves transcriptional profiles more accurately than alternative approaches, establishing best practices for single-cell studies of neural tissue.

## Key Highlights

- Systematic evaluation of fixation methods for scRNA-seq
- Methanol fixation preserves transcriptional integrity
- Application to neural cell types
- Protocol optimization for droplet-based platforms
