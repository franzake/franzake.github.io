I develop open-source bioinformatics software and computational pipelines for transcriptomics analysis. My work emphasizes reproducibility, scalability, and adherence to community standards.

## Major Projects

### SCALPEL

**Single-Cell Alternative PoLyadenylation and Isoform ExpLoration**

SCALPEL is a comprehensive Nextflow pipeline designed for single-cell isoform quantification and alternative polyadenylation analysis. The pipeline integrates state-of-the-art tools for:

- Full-length transcript reconstruction from scRNA-seq data
- Isoform-level quantification at single-cell resolution
- Alternative polyadenylation site identification and usage quantification
- Differential isoform usage analysis across cell types and conditions
- Quality control and visualization modules

SCALPEL follows nf-core design principles, ensuring reproducibility across HPC and cloud environments.

**Status**: Under development  
**Technologies**: Nextflow, Docker/Singularity, R, Python

### RNA-seq Fusion Detection Workflows

I contribute to computational workflows for fusion transcript detection in cancer genomics:

- **STAR-Fusion integration**: Scalable fusion detection pipelines
- **nf-core/rnafusion contributions**: Community-driven RNA fusion analysis workflows
- **Benchmarking studies**: Comparative evaluation of fusion detection tools

These workflows enable robust identification of gene fusions from bulk and single-cell RNA-seq data, supporting cancer diagnostics and translational research.

**Technologies**: STAR-Fusion, Arriba, FusionCatcher, Nextflow
