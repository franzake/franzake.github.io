## Active Research Projects

### Multi-omics Integration in Cancer Pathology

Integrating spatial transcriptomics, single-cell RNA-seq, and genomic data to characterize tumor heterogeneity and disease-associated molecular programs. This work aims to identify therapeutic targets and biomarkers for precision oncology.

**Collaborators**: Dr. Vivek Charu (Stanford Pathology)  
**Funding**: Stanford University  
**Period**: 2025–Present

---

### SCALPEL: Single-Cell APA Analysis Pipeline

Development of a comprehensive Nextflow pipeline for alternative polyadenylation analysis at single-cell resolution. SCALPEL enables researchers to quantify APA dynamics across cell types and identify regulatory mechanisms controlling 3' UTR isoform selection.

**Status**: Under development  
**Technologies**: Nextflow, nf-core, R, Python  
**Period**: 2023–Present

---

### Isoform-Level Analysis of Single-Cell Transcriptomes

Computational methods for transcript isoform quantification and differential usage analysis in single-cell datasets. This project addresses statistical and computational challenges in resolving isoform-level expression from sparse single-cell data.

**Institution**: University of Barcelona / IDIBELL  
**Supervisor**: Dr. Mireya Plass  
**Period**: 2021–2025

---

### Fusion Transcript Detection in Cancer

Development and benchmarking of computational workflows for gene fusion detection from RNA-seq data. Contributions to nf-core/rnafusion and integration of multiple fusion detection tools for improved sensitivity and specificity.

**Collaboration**: nf-core community  
**Period**: 2022–Present
